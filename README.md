## 🧠 About Me

My academic background is in Pharmacology and Therapeutics, with industrial exposure to pharmaceutical production, quality control and quality assurance.

During my industrial training at **Ranbaxy Nigeria (Sun Pharma)**, I gained exposure to pharmaceutical manufacturing processes, laboratory quality-control procedures and quality-assurance practices. I subsequently completed my NYSC at the Pharmaceutical Department of the Ekiti State Ministry of Health.

My growing research interests include:

- Neurodegenerative diseases and their molecular mechanisms
- Neuroinflammation and glial-cell biology
- Molecular and cellular neuroscience
- Neuropharmacology and neuroprotective drug discovery
- Computational biology and bioinformatics
- Computer-Aided Drug Design (CADD)
- Translational biomedical research

I am particularly interested in connecting computational findings with experimental evidence to better understand disease mechanisms, investigate potential therapeutic targets and explore drug discovery opportunities.
